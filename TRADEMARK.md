# KaspaVerse branding policy

**The software is open source. The name and the mark are not part of that grant.**

KaspaVerse's source code is licensed under the ISC License (`LICENSE`). That licence is a
grant of rights in **copyright**: use, copy, modify, distribute. It says nothing about
trademarks, and it is not a licence to use the project's name or identity. This document
states how the two differ and what the project asks of you. It is a **project policy**, not
a legal instrument, and it does not create rights that do not otherwise exist.

## Status, stated plainly

No trademark is registered. No legal entity holds these marks today. The project is
maintained by its author, and the repository is expected to move to an official KaspaVerse
organization later; this policy is written so it can be aligned with that structure when it
exists, rather than to assert something that does not.

Nothing here should be read as a claim of a registered trademark, and no registration
symbol is used anywhere in this project.

## What the project treats as its marks

- The name **KaspaVerse**, and confusingly similar variants.
- The **KaspaVerse mark** — the orb-and-inverted-K logo, its glow treatment, and the app
  icon built from them.
- Product and surface names used as identifiers of the official project.

The *files* that draw the mark are source code and are covered by the ISC License like
everything else. Copying that code is permitted. Using the resulting mark **as a badge of
origin** — to identify a product as being KaspaVerse — is what this policy is about.

## What you may do

- Use, fork, modify and redistribute the code, commercially or not, under the ISC License.
- Say truthfully what your software is built from: *"a fork of KaspaVerse"*, *"based on
  KaspaVerse"*, *"compatible with KaspaVerse"*. Nominative, descriptive reference is fine
  and always was.
- Write about, teach, review or criticise the project by name.
- Reproduce the logo when referring to the project itself, unaltered, in articles, talks and
  documentation.

## What the project asks you not to do

- **Do not present a fork or derivative as an official KaspaVerse release.** Publishing
  ISC-licensed code does not make your build ours, and the licence does not say it does.
- Do not use the name or mark as the name, icon or store listing of your own distribution
  in a way that suggests it is published, endorsed or reviewed by the project.
- Do not use the name or mark for a service, token, sale or fundraise implying official
  status. This matters more here than in most projects: the software handles other people's
  money, and a user who trusts the wrong build loses funds.
- Do not modify the logo and continue to call it the KaspaVerse mark.
- Do not register the name, the mark, or confusingly similar variants as your own.

## If you fork

Rename. Ship under your own name and your own icon, keep the ISC copyright notice and this
project's attribution intact (`NOTICE.md`), and state the relationship in your own words.
That satisfies the licence and this policy at once.

## Changes

This policy may change as the project's ownership structure is formalised. Its intent will
not: **the code is free to use, and the name is how users tell the official build from
everything else.**

Questions about a specific use: open an issue at
`https://github.com/Drcryptodee/kaspaverse`.
